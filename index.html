About BCBA

A research-led approach to care business advisory

Bragg Care Business Advisory (BCBA) is built on a research-led approach to advisory work: careful research, close reading of complex regulatory and tender documents, and clear, evidence-based writing underpin everything we do.

We pair that foundation with structured, ongoing specialist training in health and social care regulation, childcare and early years compliance, public sector commissioning, and bid writing — and work closely with Second Chance Financial Ltd on the funding side, so clients get proposal writing, compliance guidance and funding support from one advisory relationship.

  • Structured, ongoing training in CQC, Ofsted and early years compliance
  • Specialist bid and tender writing for public sector contracts
  • Direct partnership with Second Chance Financial Ltd for funding applications
